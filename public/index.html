Light pairs

Each pair is one Govee controller: one IP, two lights you control separately. Type the IP, or scan and pick a slot. Empty slots still show in the preview.

Bluetooth pairs are BRMesh-app lights, sent through an ESP32 bridge (deploy/esp32-brmesh.yaml). C9 relay boards switch plain strings on and off through an ESP32 (deploy/esp32-relays.yaml). For both, type the ESP32's IP and its names. A smart plug (Tuya: Enbrighten, Smart Life) switches whatever is plugged into it on and off with the show, zone and schedule; it needs its device ID and local key (README "Smart plug").
